## 1. Exalted (2e, 3e, Essence): motes, Essence rating, pools, anima banner, Charms, Excellencies, stunts, and 3e Initiative

### Takeaway
Exalted separates three things the Cosmo design merges. **Essence** is a slow-growing permanent rating that gates which Charms you can learn. **Motes** are the spendable fuel, split into Personal and Peripheral pools. **Anima** is the visible aura that flares automatically as you spend Peripheral motes. Anima is a *cost* (loss of stealth and subtlety) as well as a spectacle, and at its top level it unlocks iconic powers. The closest match to "Cosmo that rises during a fight" is not motes but 3e's **Initiative**: a combat resource that grows by landing attacks and draining the enemy's, then is cashed in for a decisive blow.

### Cited Findings
**Pools and Essence (3e)**
- 3e Solar pools at character creation: **Personal = Essence × 3 + 10** and **Peripheral = Essence × 7 + 26**. At Essence 1 that is 13 Personal and 33 Peripheral motes. — [StartPlaying: Creating a Character for Exalted 3e](https://startplaying.games/blog/posts/creating-a-character-for-exalted-3rd-edition); [Mokura's Exalted chargen cheat sheet](http://mokura.wikidot.com/exalted-charagen-cheat-sheet) (both appeared in the result set that gave this formula)
- In 3e, mote recovery "is no longer dependent on stunts". Essence instead "returns at a fixed rate of **5 motes per round in combat**". The same source says stunts no longer regenerate Essence but still add dice. — [Onyx Path: Exalted Third Edition Mechanics Overview](https://theonyxpath.com/exalted-third-edition-mechanics-overview/). (This page is a pre-release overview. It also claims Willpower "can no longer be regained by performing stunts", which may conflict with the published book; see Gaps.)

**Anima banner (3e)**
- When a character spends **5 or more Peripheral motes in an instant, her anima rises one level for every 5 Peripheral motes** spent. — [Green Sun Rising (Obsidian Portal): Essence and Anima](https://green-sun-rising.obsidianportal.com/wikis/essence-and-anima)
- The same source describes the levels as: Dim/Glowing (the caste mark glows like a candle), then Burning (a swirling nimbus in the Exaltation's colours), then Iconic (a personalized display that "proclaims their legend to the world"). **The banner drops one level at the end of each scene.** — [Green Sun Rising: Essence and Anima](https://green-sun-rising.obsidianportal.com/wikis/essence-and-anima)
- Anima can be suppressed at a price. When a Night Caste spends Peripheral Essence she can stop it from feeding her anima: **+1 mote for non-Obvious Charms, or double cost for Obvious Charms**. — [White Wolf Wiki: Night Caste](https://whitewolf.fandom.com/wiki/Night_Caste)

**Anima banner (2e): mote thresholds**
- The 2e banner is keyed to Peripheral motes spent: **1–3 motes**, caste mark glitters (normal difficulty to spot); **4–7**, caste mark burns (impossible to hide; a +2 difficulty modifier is mentioned); **8–10**, coruscant aura; **11–15**, brilliant bonfire with totemic imagery; **16+**, iconic banner. The iconic anima power triggers automatically at the 16+ level. — [Fixalted: Second Edition Solar Anima Powers](http://fixalted.bazzalisk.org/index.php?title=Second_Edition_Solar_Anima_Powers); [White Wolf Wiki: No Moon Caste](https://whitewolf.fandom.com/wiki/No_Moon_Caste) (result set for this table)

**Initiative as a rising combat resource (3e): the strongest match for rising Cosmo**
- 3e Initiative is "not just the order in which the participants take their turns – it's a resource". It abstracts battle momentum. — [Exalted 3E Combat Cheat Sheet (Obsidian Portal)](https://exalted3e.obsidianportal.com/wikis/combat-cheat-sheet); [Ekorren, "Exalted 3E: The Basics of Combat" (Patreon)](https://www.patreon.com/ekorren/posts/manuscript-3e-of-28262156)
- **Withering attacks** give the attacker +1 Initiative for hitting, plus as much Initiative as they strip from the target. **Decisive attacks** ignore weapon stats and spend Initiative to deal real harm. — [Exalted 3E Combat Cheat Sheet](https://exalted3e.obsidianportal.com/wikis/combat-cheat-sheet); [madletter combat guide PDF](http://madletter.net/rpg/exalted/CombatGuideSheet_v2.pdf)
- A target driven to **Initiative 0 or below enters Crash**. The attacker gets **+5 Initiative Break** bonus, and the crashed character cannot make decisive attacks. **If the crashed character crashes their attacker back, they instantly return to base Initiative 3, add a new Join Battle roll, and make an immediate extra attack.** — same sources
- A **missed decisive attack** does not reset Initiative. Instead it costs **3 Initiative if you have 11 or more, otherwise 2**, so you can crash yourself. — same sources

**Exalted Essence (the streamlined one-book edition)**
- Anima is a numeric track. "**2 motes get you 1 anima**". **At 2 anima you get your Active Anima effect, and at 10 your Iconic anima effect.** At 10 "you glow so much it can be seen on the horizon and a giant iconic image appears above you." — [Onyx Path Forums: Exalted Essence Questions](https://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/main-category/exalted/1454140-exalted-essence-questions)
- The anima display fades one level per scene along the steps **10 > 7 > 5 > 3 > 1 > 0**, and some charms reduce it faster. — [Onyx Path Forums: Exalted Essence Questions](https://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/main-category/exalted/1454140-exalted-essence-questions)
- Design shift: "in previous editions, players had to keep anima down, but in Essence the system is built so that it is very difficult to keep it down". A player reads this as being "limited to essentially 8 motes or 6 motes unless you are willing to flare anima". — [Onyx Path Forums: Exalted Essence Questions](https://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/main-category/exalted/1454140-exalted-essence-questions)

### Inferences
- **Two-track model worth copying.** Exalted keeps a *slow* track (Essence rating, which gates Charm access) apart from a *fast* track (motes spent in the scene, and Initiative in the round). "Sense = tier, Cosmo = in-fight resource" is the same split. Exalted suggests the tier should gate *which techniques exist* (like Charm Essence minimums). The fast resource should gate *how hard you can push them this round*.
- **The aura should be a mechanical consequence, not only flavour.** In every edition, spending energy automatically raises a visible level with side effects: stealth is lost, and signature powers unlock at the top. A "Cosmo aura" track can follow the same pattern. Spending or burning Cosmo raises the aura. At high aura the Saint gets their "constellation image" effect (the parallel to Exalted's Iconic) but can no longer hide or feint.
- **Initiative is a ready template for "Cosmo rises during the fight".** You grow it by trading blows, you steal it from the opponent, and a comeback rule gives a crashed fighter a reset, a fresh roll and a free attack. That comeback rule is literally "fall, get up, strike back". Initiative is also the damage pool for the decisive strike, which matches the pillar "Cosmo = number of dice".
- **Exalted Essence's anima steps (10 > 7 > 5 > 3 > 1)** show that a decay curve which drops fast at high levels and slowly at low levels keeps the top-tier state rare and brief.

### Gaps
- Could not verify the published 3e stunt rewards. Unverified recollection: 1-point stunt +2 dice; 2-point +2 dice and +1 Willpower (or +1 static value); 3-point +2 dice and +2 Willpower. This conflicts with the pre-release Onyx overview quoted above.
- Could not verify the 3e Solar Excellency costs. Unverified recollection: 1 mote per die, capped at Attribute + Ability; 2 motes per +1 static value.
- Could not verify 3e Essence advancement. Unverified recollection: Essence rises automatically at total-XP milestones rather than being bought. Also that Charms carry Ability and Essence minimums plus prerequisite Charms in trees.
- Could not verify the 2e stunt/mote refund rules or the 2e Excellencies (First/Second/Third). Also could not verify 3e's exact level names (Dim, Glowing, Burning, Bonfire/Iconic). The Obsidian Portal source seems to merge Dim and Glowing.
- Exalted Essence mote pool size, Excellency changes and stunt rules: not found.

## 2. Tenra Bansho Zero: Aiki, Kiai, Karma/Kegare, and the Asura risk

### Takeaway
In Tenra Bansho Zero (TBZ) the fuel does not come from the fiction's damage. It comes from the **audience**: other players hand out Aiki chits for cool, Fate-driven play. Aiki converts to Kiai, which buys huge boosts (20–30 dice, extra attacks, higher skills). Every Kiai spent becomes **Karma**, and ending an Intermission over **108 Karma** turns the character into an unplayable **Asura**. Power is literally corruption, and the only way back is to cut or rewrite your attachments (Fates).

### Cited Findings
- Aiki is awarded when a player "play[s] to their Fates, do[es] something particularly cool or dramatic, or otherwise kick[s] the game up to the next level". — [TV Tropes: Tenra Bansho Zero](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/TabletopGame/TenraBanshoZERO); [RPGnet: TBZ chit economy](https://forum.rpg.net/index.php?threads/tenra-bansho-zero-chit-economy.906996/)
- **Audience method:** "each player keeps a side reserve of **three Aiki chits per scene**", and any other player can award one when a character does something, with "**no more than one Aiki per action**". **Scene Judge method (alternative):** the GM picks one player per scene to be the sole arbiter of Aiki. — [ProfessorProf's TBZ writeup](https://writeups.letsyouandhimfight.com/professorprof/tenra-bansho-zero/); [RPGnet: TBZ rules questions](https://forum.rpg.net/threads/tenra-bansho-zero-some-rules-questions.662678/)
- The economy loop: spend Aiki in Intermissions to gain Kiai. Spend Kiai to become more powerful, which raises Karma. Sublimate or rewrite Fates to lower Karma. Spend leftover Aiki on new Fates (**1 Aiki per new Fate**). — [RPGnet: TBZ chit economy](https://forum.rpg.net/index.php?threads/tenra-bansho-zero-chit-economy.906996/); [ProfessorProf's TBZ writeup](https://writeups.letsyouandhimfight.com/professorprof/tenra-bansho-zero/)
- The conversion step as summarized: "During intermission, you roll **Aiki x Empathy dice vs your highest Destiny**, with successes turning into Kiai". **Aiki can also be spent directly as Kiai, which avoids the usual Karma increase.** — [ProfessorProf's TBZ writeup](https://writeups.letsyouandhimfight.com/professorprof/tenra-bansho-zero/) (summary wording; the exact procedure needs checking)
- Kiai buys bonus dice, a temporary skill boost for a roll, or automatic successes. It "can easily boost rolls to **20 or 30 dice**, not to mention buying **extra attacks** and a host of other options". — [Mythcreants: Tenra Bansho Zero Is Fun and Bizarre](https://mythcreants.com/blog/tenra-bansho-zero-is-fun-insanity/)
- Skill-rank purchase with Kiai: "**10/20/40 Kiai to raise a skill by 1 rank**". One summary line also gives "5 Kiai to raise a General Skill from 1 to 2". — [Mythcreants](https://mythcreants.com/blog/tenra-bansho-zero-is-fun-insanity/); [Everyday Magical Girls wiki: TBZ](http://everydaymagicalgirls.wikidot.com/tenra-bansho-zero) (the two numbers may be different uses or editions; unresolved)
- "**Every point of Kiai you spend is added to your Karma.**" Karma represents "the pollution of the character's soul or the strength of the character's ties to the world of flesh and suffering". Higher Karma makes the character "crazier" and less restrained. — [TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/TabletopGame/TenraBanshoZERO); [Mythcreants](https://mythcreants.com/blog/tenra-bansho-zero-is-fun-insanity/)
- "If you end an Intermission with **more than 108 Karma**, you become an **Asura** and are relegated to NPC status". The Asura is called "unplayable". — [TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/TabletopGame/TenraBanshoZERO); [RPGnet sell-me thread](https://forum.rpg.net/index.php?threads/tell-me-about-sell-me-on-tenra-bansho-zero.735393/)
- To shed Karma you "discard or rewrite a Fate". Severing attachments lowers Karma "but also changes the character's outlook". — [RPGnet sell-me thread](https://forum.rpg.net/index.php?threads/tell-me-about-sell-me-on-tenra-bansho-zero.735393/); [The Game Mechanic blog](http://powerframe-rpg.blogspot.com/2012/10/tenra-bansho-zero-prelude.html)
- Annelidists (hosts of symbiotic insects) pay for many abilities with **health**, "feed[ing] tiny parts of themselves ... in exchange for power". — [RPGnet sell-me thread](https://forum.rpg.net/index.php?threads/tell-me-about-sell-me-on-tenra-bansho-zero.735393/)

### Inferences
- **The audience economy fits "allies in danger / the crowd cheers the Saint on" very well.** Proposal: each player gets 3 "Cosmo sparks" per scene to give to *another* player's Saint for a dramatic moment (max 1 per action). This makes "rising Cosmo" a social act rather than only a damage trigger.
- **Deferred cost is what makes big buys acceptable.** TBZ lets a character go huge now (20–30 dice) because the price is paid later (Karma, then checked at Intermission). "Burning Cosmo costs HP" is an *immediate* cost. A hybrid is possible: HP now, plus a slow track (like Karma) that can end a Saint's career.
- **The Fates-lower-Karma loop** mirrors the Saint Seiya theme of fighting for others. Bonds become both the source of power and the way to shed its corruption.
- **The HP-for-power precedent (Annelidists)** supports the "burn Cosmo at the cost of HP" pillar.

### Gaps
- I could not find a reliable, complete **Kiai spending menu** with exact costs (dice per Kiai, automatic success cost, extra action cost, "keep acting while dying" type buys). The sources only say these options exist.
- **Kegare:** no source defined a separate Kegare mechanic apart from Karma. It may be the in-setting word for impurity, or a subsystem for specific archetypes; unresolved.
- The exact Aiki → Kiai conversion procedure (the "Aiki × Empathy vs Destiny" summary line) needs checking against the rulebook.

## 3. Double Cross (3rd ed.): Encroachment Rate, threshold bonuses, Lois/Titus, Backtrack

### Takeaway
In Double Cross, risk is the fuel. Encroachment rises by **1d10 every time you enter a scene** plus the cost of each power. At **60% / 80% / 100%** it grants **+1 / +2 / +3 dice**, and at 100% also **+1 effect level**. So the closer you drift to the edge, the stronger you get. After the climax, each surviving **Lois** (bond) lets you roll 1d10 to pull Encroachment back down. Ending **over 100%** means the character becomes a monster (Gjaum) and is lost. Players are paid in XP for ending *close to* the line, so they are pushed to flirt with it.

### Cited Findings
- "Each scene adds **1D10** to a player's Encroachment rate." The rate represents how far the Renegade virus has taken over. — [Faustusnotes: Double Cross 3: Titus and Lois](https://faustusnotes.com/2010/07/25/japanese-rpg-double-cross-3-titus-and-lois/); [Panda Gaming Grove review](https://pandagaminggrove.blogspot.com/2013/08/double-cross.html)
- High Encroachment "boosts the character's power levels and dice pool size, but also makes it harder to survive the final Backtrack". "At certain Encroachment thresholds you actually get extra dice and level boosts to most of your Renegade powers." — [Doresh's Double Cross Advanced Rulebook writeup](https://writeups.letsyouandhimfight.com/doresh/double-cross--advanced-rulebook/); [TV Tropes: Double Cross](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/TabletopGame/DoubleCross)
- Threshold bonuses from Japanese sources: **60% → +1 die, 80% → +2 dice, 100% → +3 dice**, and **100% also gives +1 effect level**. — [ダブルクロスのススメ: はじめてのダブルクロス・ルールサマリー](https://caunserahomare.hatenablog.com/entry/2023/01/01/205908); [FEAR official DX3 record sheet with the 侵蝕率効果表 (Encroachment effect table)](http://www.fear.co.jp/dbx3rd/download/dc3_recordsheet.pdf); [Qiita: chat-palette macro so you don't forget the Encroachment bonus](https://qiita.com/crabkawazu/items/1d032b93c47649363537)
- Japanese community material confirms Encroachment can go **well past 100% in play**, with extra breakpoints and effect-level boosts above that. — [ダブルクロスのススメ: 侵蝕率はどこまで上げられるの？ (how high can Encroachment go?)](https://caunserahomare.hatenablog.com/entry/2018/04/03/204146); [ダブルクロスのススメ: effects that raise Effect LV](https://caunserahomare.hatenablog.com/entry/2023/08/02/235601)
- **Loises** are bonds with people the hero protects or cares about. **Tituses** are dead, broken or antagonistic bonds. A Lois can be **sublimated** into a Titus for a one-shot, very powerful benefit, "stuff like a **10 die bonus**, the ability to **instantly recover from being brought to zero hitpoints**". — [Faustusnotes](https://faustusnotes.com/2010/07/25/japanese-rpg-double-cross-3-titus-and-lois/); [Deeper in the Game: Double Cross RPG](https://bankuei.wordpress.com/2014/02/12/double-cross-rpg/)
- "Each Lois you have at the end of a session allows you to reduce your Encroachment by **1D10**." Maximum **7 Loises**, but only **3 carry over** between sessions. — [Cyphoderus's Double Cross writeup](https://writeups.letsyouandhimfight.com/cyphoderus/double-cross/)
- Advanced Rulebook: the **E-Loises** of defeated enemies become **bonus dice** for Backtrack. This offsets the loss of sublimated Loises (Tituses) and lets players who have no Tituses be "more spam-happy". — [Doresh's Advanced Rulebook writeup](https://writeups.letsyouandhimfight.com/doresh/double-cross--advanced-rulebook/)
- If Encroachment is **over 100% after Backtrack**, the PC becomes a Gjaum (an NPC) and a new character is needed. — [TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/TabletopGame/DoubleCross); [Panda Gaming Grove](https://pandagaminggrove.blogspot.com/2013/08/double-cross.html)
- XP by final Encroachment: "Ending the session at **0–30% is worth a mere 2 points**, but going all the way to **70–99% is worth 5 XP** (going over **100% is worth only 3**)". "The closer you can skirt to 100% ... without going over, the greater your reward." — [Panda Gaming Grove](https://pandagaminggrove.blogspot.com/2013/08/double-cross.html); [Cyphoderus's writeup](https://writeups.letsyouandhimfight.com/cyphoderus/double-cross/)

### Inferences
- **The best structural analogue for "Cosmo rises and becomes dangerous".** One number does three jobs: it is the power multiplier (dice and levels), the clock (1d10 per scene means it always rises), and the danger meter (the check at the end). The Saint Seiya version could be: Cosmo rises per scene or trigger; thresholds add dice and technique levels; above the "Seventh Sense line" you are stronger but at risk (burnout, Cosmo exhaustion, losing senses, death) in an end-of-battle roll.
- **Bonds as the brakes.** Loises are the mechanical reason friendship saves you. That is exactly Saint Seiya's theme (Athena and fellow Saints). "Friends in danger" could raise Cosmo during the fight *and* be the dice that bring you back afterwards.
- **Titus is the "burn" button.** It is a one-shot, huge boost (10 dice, or getting up from 0 HP) bought by permanently spending a bond. That maps well onto "burning Cosmo to jump a tier" as a once-per-arc move with a lasting cost.
- **Pay XP for living near the edge.** This turns avoiding risk into a mild penalty and stops players from sandbagging.

### Gaps
- Exact bonuses above 100% were not confirmed. Unverified recollection of the DX3 table: 130–159% +4 dice; 160%+ +5 dice and +2 effect levels; further dice at 200%/240%/300%.
- Starting Encroachment values (set by Breed/Works; recalled as roughly 30-something), the Resurrect/"rise at 0 HP" mechanic, and the optional "double the dice per Lois at an XP cost" Backtrack option were not confirmed.
- The Double Cross English translation's status and edition naming (3rd edition vs. Double Cross 3rd Edition Revised) were not verified.

## 4. Anima: Beyond Fantasy: Ki points, accumulation per turn, techniques, Inhumanity/Zen

### Takeaway
Anima models ki as a pool that must be **accumulated during combat, turn by turn**, drawn from each characteristic. Accumulation is halved if you do anything else that round. Techniques are custom-built from effects and bought with **Martial Knowledge (MK)**. Anima also caps achievable results by tier: normally nothing above **Impossible (280)**. **Inhumanity** unlocks **Inhuman (320)** and **Zen** unlocks **Zen (440)**. This is a clean "you need the next Sense to break this limit" mechanic.

### Cited Findings
- Ki Points come from **Strength, Dexterity, Agility, Constitution, Power and Willpower**. **1 Ki point per characteristic point up to 10, and 2 per point above 10**. — [d20 Radio: Building a Ki Character (Anima)](https://www.d20radio.com/main/rogue-character-anima-beyond-fantasy-building-a-ki-character/); [Anima wiki: Ki Dominion](https://animarpgbeyondfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Ki_Dominion)
- **Ki Accumulation** is a per-characteristic rate of concentrating Ki. Its base comes from the characteristics. Ki Points and Accumulation are raised with Development Points (cost depends on class), **not** with MK. — [d20 Radio](https://www.d20radio.com/main/rogue-character-anima-beyond-fantasy-building-a-ki-character/); [Anima wiki: Ki Dominion](https://animarpgbeyondfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Ki_Dominion)
- "Accumulating Ki is a **Passive Action**. When characters perform other Actions in the same round, they must reduce their Accumulation to **half, rounding up**." Characters "can use as many Ki Points per turn as their Accumulation allows", from all or only some characteristics. — [FFG forum archive: Ki Accumulation](https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/1207-ki-accumulation/)
- Out of combat, spent Ki recovers at **1 point per hour per characteristic, doubled when meditating**. — [FFG forum archive: Ki Accumulation](https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/1207-ki-accumulation/)
- **MK** is earned by level (per class progression), by the Martial Mastery advantage, or by mastering martial arts. It can only buy Ki Abilities and Techniques. — [Anima wiki: Ki Dominion](https://animarpgbeyondfantasy.fandom.com/wiki/Ki_Dominion)
- For technique-focused characters, "Ki Accumulation is your LIFE-BLOOD". — [d20 Radio](https://www.d20radio.com/main/rogue-character-anima-beyond-fantasy-building-a-ki-character/)
- Difficulty ladder top end: **Impossible 280**, **Inhuman 320**, **Zen 440**. "You can only get a net result above 280 in Physical skills if you have **Inhumanity** (and above 320 if you have **Zen**)." — [Ariamaki's Anima writeup](https://writeups.letsyouandhimfight.com/ariamakizeetoo/anima-beyond-fantasy/); [GiantITP Anima thread](https://forums.giantitp.com/showthread.php?p=21426517)
- The official Anima Project web supplement lists "**280 · Imposible · 30 intensidades · 320 · Inhumano · 40 intensidades · 440 · Zen**", pairing difficulty tiers with elemental "intensities". — [Anima Project: ABF Complemento Web Vol. 1 (PDF)](https://www.animaproject.studio/ABF_Complemento_Web_Vol1.pdf)
- There is dedicated community discussion of Inhumanity/Zen as tier-breakers. — [FFG forum archive: On Inhumanity and Zen](https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/68608-on-inhumanity-and-zen/)

### Inferences
- **Per-turn accumulation is a direct model for "Cosmo rises during the fight"**, with a built-in trade-off: charge up (full rate) or act (half rate). This is the classic anime "powering up while the enemy talks" beat, and it creates tactical choices without extra triggers.
- **Inhumanity/Zen is the Senses mechanic.** A hard *ceiling* on results that only a tier unlock removes is cleaner than stacking bonuses. Proposal: "Without the Seventh Sense, no action can exceed X, or you cannot move faster than Mach Y." Awakening the Sense mid-fight removes the cap. Speed tiers in Saint Seiya (Bronze sub-sonic to Mach, Gold at light speed) map naturally onto result caps.
- **Techniques built from effects (MK plus Ki cost)** suggest a way to let players design signature Saint techniques ("Pegasus Ryu Sei Ken") from a menu, with the Sense tier gating which effects are available.

### Gaps
- The exact Ki Accumulation table (accumulation per characteristic value) was not found.
- Technique construction specifics (levels 1/2/3, MK costs, Ki costs per effect, maintenance) were not found. Dominus Exxet (the ki supplement) is the source to check.
- MK costs and prerequisites for Inhumanity and Zen, and the full list of Ki Abilities (Use of Ki, Ki Control, Aura Extension, Presence Extrusion, etc.), were not verified.

## 5. Scion 2nd Edition (Storypath): Legend, Tiers, Momentum, Scale; plus the 1e contrast

### Takeaway
Scion 2e groups power into **Tiers**: Origin; Hero (Legend 1–4); Demigod (5–8); God (9–12). Legend is gained through **deeds, not XP**. Tier gaps are handled with **Scale**: each step of Scale over an opponent gives **+2 Enhancement** (automatic successes). Two Scaled beings cancel down to the difference. A Demigod can **spend 1 Legend to temporarily raise Scale** for an action that fits a Title. This is almost exactly "burn Cosmo to jump a tier for one action". Momentum is a **shared party pool** fed by failure and complications.

### Cited Findings
- Tiers: Origin, Hero, Demigod, God. "Those with **Legend 1 through 4 are heroes**, those with **Legend 5 through 8 are demigods**, and those with **Legend 9 through 12**" are for Scion: God. — [It Came From The Bookshelf: Scion 2e Demigod](http://www.itcamefromthebookshelf.com/2022/10/scion-2e-demigod.html); [Mephit James: Scion Demigod review](https://mephitjamesblog.wordpress.com/2022/07/19/scion-demigod-review/)
- Legend "is **no longer increased by the expenditure of experience points but directly by the accomplishment of deeds**". — [Fantasy-Faction: Scion 2e review, part one](https://fantasy-faction.com/2019/scion-rpg-2nd-edition-review-part-one-scion-origin)
- "Characters gain **Scale equal to [Legend/2], rounded up**", used for **Feats of Scale** that let Demigods perform godly actions. — [Scion 2e Demigod manuscript (Scribd)](https://www.scribd.com/document/502373304/Scion-2e-Demigod-Manuscript); [Mephit James review](https://mephitjamesblog.wordpress.com/2022/07/19/scion-demigod-review/)
- Feat of Scale: for an action covered by a **Title**, "spend **one Legend** to increase Scale by **(Legend/2, round up)** for that action". For an action covered by a keyword but not a Title, "spend **one Legend and two Momentum** to increase Scale by **one**". — [Onyx Path Forums: Scion Q&A thread](https://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/main-category/main-forum/scion/1224340-ask-a-question-get-an-answer-scion-edition/page2); [Onyx Path Forums: [Sc 2E] Questions on Scale](https://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/main-category/main-forum/scion/1437449-sc-2e-questions-on-scale)
- "When you have scale above the target of an attack, you gain **+2 enhancements per scale above**." If both sides have Scale, "the difference in values is treated as the Scale of the larger opponent (e.g., **Scale 2 vs. 3 is treated as Scale 0 vs. 1**)". Scale "most often it can be dealt narratively". — [Onyx Path Forums: Questions on Scale](https://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/main-category/main-forum/scion/1437449-sc-2e-questions-on-scale)
- Legend can be **spent** (gone until recovered) or **imbued** (committed until the effect ends, then returned). — [Scion 2e Rules Summary (System sans Setting)](https://samhaine.wordpress.com/2018/04/09/scion-2e-rules-summary/)
- Momentum: **+1 when a Condition causes a failure or setback, +1 for resolving a Condition**. Spend **1 Momentum = 1 die**, extend a Complex action by one interval, or power Knacks. — [Scion 2e Wiki: Cheatsheet](https://scion-origin.fandom.com/wiki/Cheatsheet)
- Momentum pool size (**conflicting summaries**): one says the group "starts each session with **one point of Momentum per member**" and the pool "cannot exceed **two points per member**". Another says it "normally starts empty and holds up to **12**" and drops to **6** at each new scene. Knack spending from the communal pool should be agreed by the group. — [Scion 2e Rules Summary (System sans Setting)](https://samhaine.wordpress.com/2018/04/09/scion-2e-rules-summary/); [Scion 2e quick reference (gwenscode)](https://gwenscode.com/writing/rpg/scion2e-quick-reference.pdf). These may reflect different books or drafts (Origin/Hero vs. later). Unresolved.
- Community concern about the Scale rules exists; see the "Scale Problems" thread. — [Onyx Path Forums: Scale Problems](https://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/main-category/main-forum/scion/1235364-scale-problems)
- **Scion 1e contrast (exponential curve):** the Legend point pool is **Legend dots squared**. Epic Attributes give **automatic successes** at the heroic level: **1 dot = 1, 2 dots = 2, 3 dots = 4**. — [White Wolf Wiki: Epic Attribute](https://whitewolf.fandom.com/wiki/Epic_Attribute); [Scion: Hero to Ragnarok: Epic Attributes (Obsidian Portal)](https://scion-hero-to-ragnarok.obsidianportal.com/wikis/epic-attributes)

### Inferences
- **The Feat of Scale is the cleanest published precedent for "burn Cosmo to jump a tier temporarily".** It costs a scarce resource, lasts one action, and is gated by identity (Title). That parallels "only when fighting for what the Saint believes in / their constellation's theme".
- **Relative Scale ("2 vs 3 = 0 vs 1")** means only the *difference* between tiers matters. A Gold vs Gold fight plays like Bronze vs Bronze. A Bronze who reaches the Seventh Sense vs a Gold plays as an even fight. That is the right shape for Saint Seiya.
- **+2 Enhancement per step is a linear, bounded gap.** It keeps underdogs alive, unlike Scion 1e's near-exponential automatic successes, which made tier gaps crushing. If Sense = die size, check that the gap per Sense step is not so large that no amount of Cosmo can bridge it.
- **Momentum from failure** fits the pillar "Cosmo rises from taking damage / falling". Setbacks feed the pool, as in Momentum.

### Gaps
- Exact **Scale** rules text (Enhancement vs. alternative uses, non-combat Scale types) was not read first-hand. It comes from forum summaries.
- The Momentum pool conflict above is unresolved.
- **"Inhumanity" in Scion 2e:** I found nothing indicating Scion 2e uses a mechanic called Inhumanity. The term matches Anima's Inhumanity/Zen (section 4). Scion 2e's gap mechanics found were Scale, Feats of Scale and Legend. Also unverified recollection: the Scion 1e Epic Attribute progression beyond 3 dots (1, 2, 4, 7, 11, 16, 22, 29, 37, 46).

## 6. Godbound (Sine Nomine): Effort, Fray die, damage chart, lesser vs worthy foes, mobs, Dominion

### Takeaway
Godbound handles the god-versus-mortal gap by **switching resolution modes** rather than scaling numbers. Enemies with Hit Dice at or below your level are **"lesser foes"**. Your free **Fray die (d8)** shreds them every round, and damage against them is counted in *Hit Dice*, not HP. Only **worthy foes** get a real fight. **Effort** is not mana. It is a cap on how many powers you can keep running at once (commit for the scene or day).

### Cited Findings
- **Effort** is "the well of divine energy a Godbound can draw on to pass failed saves or use certain gifts". You can **commit Effort for the scene** (or day). — [Godbound? (D&D Beyond forums)](https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/off-topic/adohands-kitchen/37219-godbound); [Seed of Worlds: Review: Godbound](https://seedofworlds.blogspot.com/2022/02/review-godbound.html)
- "Unless a gift says to commit energy for a certain period (a scene, a day), you regain that Effort after using it. **It's a limit on how many things you can have going rather than a mana system.**" — [Just The Rules: Godbound (In My Campaign)](https://www.kjd-imc.org/blog/just-the-rules-godbound/); [Age of Ravens: Godbound Redux](https://www.ageofravensgames.com/blog/godbound-redux)
- **Fray die:** "Each round a godbound hero can roll the Fray die and do the resulting damage to **a lesser foe in sight**." By default it is a **d8**. — [Daystar Chronicles: Godbound review part 1](http://daystarchronicles.blogspot.com/2016/03/review-actual-play-godbound-part-1.html); [RPG Writeups: Godbound (Doresh)](https://writeups.letsyouandhimfight.com/doresh/godbound/)
- "An enemy with **hit dice less than or equal to your level is a lesser foe**." "Damage done to lesser foes is not measured in hit points, but in **hit dice**." — [Just The Rules: Godbound](https://www.kjd-imc.org/blog/just-the-rules-godbound/)
- **Worthy foes** have more HD than the Godbound's level, and "Fray dice will not hurt them". "**Godbound themselves never count as lesser foes**, no matter how much more powerful their opponent is." — [RPG Writeups: Godbound (Doresh)](https://writeups.letsyouandhimfight.com/doresh/godbound/)
- **Damage chart**, applied per die: **1 or less → 0; 2–5 → 1; 6–9 → 2; 10+ → 4**. — [Godbound form-fillable character sheet (PDF)](https://githyankidiaspora.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/godbound_character_sheet_form-fillable.pdf)
- **Straight damage:** some attacks (bosses, some PC powers) deal damage "straight", read normally without the chart. — [RPG Writeups: Godbound (Doresh)](https://writeups.letsyouandhimfight.com/doresh/godbound/)
- Lesser enemies pose "no threat to a pantheon unless in very large numbers", with "fantastic **Mob** rules". — [D&D Beyond forums: Godbound?](https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/off-topic/adohands-kitchen/37219-godbound)
- **Dominion** is gained "by doing divine things", such as saving townsfolk, destroying evil and winning worshippers. **Dominion and Influence** are divine and personal authority. — [D&D Beyond forums: Godbound?](https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/off-topic/adohands-kitchen/37219-godbound)
- One summary line said "Any lesser foe that tries to physically harm you suffers 1 point of damage before their attack is resolved, with Mobs taking a 1d20 normal die". **This is probably a specific Gift's effect, not a core rule.** Treat it as unverified. — [D&D Beyond forums: Godbound?](https://www.dndbeyond.com/forums/off-topic/adohands-kitchen/37219-godbound)

### Inferences
- **"Lesser foe" is the Saint Seiya mook rule.** Foot soldiers, Specters without surplices and similar enemies should never threaten a Saint one-on-one. Once a Saint outranks them by a Sense, switch them to Fray-style resolution (auto-damage, counted in "bodies") rather than rolling each fight. Save full combat for "worthy foes": same Sense tier or higher.
- **"Godbound never count as lesser foes, however strong the enemy"** is the underdog guarantee. A PC Bronze is never a mook to a Gold. They always get a real exchange, even if heavily outmatched. A CDZ rule could say the same: "Player Saints are always worthy foes."
- **The stepped damage chart (0/1/2/4)** flattens die-size differences and tapers extreme rolls. It is relevant if Sense = die size, because a chart like this stops a d12 from trivially outdamaging a d6 while still rewarding high rolls (10+ gives double damage).
- **Effort-as-commitment** could model maintained Cosmo effects (an aura or defence held up) separately from Cosmo that is spent.

### Gaps
- Exact **Effort per level** was not confirmed. Unverified recollection: 2 at 1st level, +1 per level.
- Exact **Mob** statistics (HD by size, how they attack, how the Fray die interacts with mobs) were not retrieved.
- **Divine Wrath / Miracles** mechanics were not researched.

## 7. Dragon Ball and Naruto tabletop RPGs (official and fan): ki/chakra, transformations, power levels, one-shot avoidance

### Takeaway
The only official game found is R. Talsorian's **Dragonball Z: The Anime Adventure Game** (1999). It uses Instant Fuzion with uncapped stats and a **2.5× Super Saiyan multiplier**. Reviewers said it "breaks down when big power levels and transformations start to come up". **Multiplicative power-ups are the failure mode to avoid.** Fan systems tend to gate transformations behind **"Tier of Power" requirements** with flat attribute bonuses. Naruto d20 variants use a spell-point **chakra pool** with a **per-jutsu spend cap = caster level**, fatigue thresholds and limited in-fight recovery.

### Cited Findings
**Dragonball Z: The Anime Adventure Game (R. Talsorian Games, 1999)**

### Inferences
- **Avoid multiplicative transformations.** The 1999 DBZ game's 2.5× multiplier on uncapped stats is exactly what "breaks down" at high tiers. Awakening a Sense should add a bounded step (bigger die, higher cap), not multiply everything.
- **Spend caps per technique (Naruto d20: ≤ level per jutsu)** are a simple way to stop one-shots across tiers. For Saint Seiya: "you cannot put more Cosmo dice into one technique than your Sense allows", with burning Cosmo as the sanctioned way to exceed the cap temporarily.
- **Fatigue thresholds at ½ and ¼ of the pool** echo the classic depleted-Saint state and could layer onto Cosmo depletion.
- **"Tier of Power requirement" gating** matches the plan for Senses as tier gates for techniques and transformations (Cloth upgrades, God Cloth).

## 8. Across all systems: handling huge power gaps (Bronze vs Gold) while keeping underdog wins possible; escalation curves

### Takeaway
The systems use five tools: **(a)** compare *relative* tiers only (Scion Scale differences); **(b)** switch resolution modes for mooks while guaranteeing PCs "worthy" status (Godbound); **(c)** hard caps that only a tier-unlock removes (Anima Inhumanity/Zen); **(d)** in-fight momentum resources that can swing back (Exalted Initiative crash and reset); **(e)** risk-priced burst buttons (Double Cross Titus, TBZ Kiai → Karma, Scion Feat of Scale). Escalation comes in three shapes: **per-round accumulation** (Anima Ki; Exalted's 5 motes/round), **per-scene clocks** (Double Cross +1d10 per scene) and **triggered or earned gains** (Exalted withering hits; TBZ audience Aiki; Scion Momentum from setbacks).

### Cited Findings
- **Relative tier comparison:** Scion 2e gives +2 Enhancement per Scale step above the target, and two Scaled combatants compare only the difference (2 vs 3 = 0 vs 1). — [Onyx Path Forums: Questions on Scale](https://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/main-category/main-forum/scion/1437449-sc-2e-questions-on-scale)
- **Temporary tier jump at a cost:** Scion 2e Feat of Scale, spending 1 Legend for +(Legend/2) Scale on one Title action. — [Onyx Path Forums: Scion Q&A](https://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/main-category/main-forum/scion/1224340-ask-a-question-get-an-answer-scion-edition/page2)
- **Mode switch for mooks, PCs always worthy:** Godbound lesser foes (HD ≤ level) take Fray damage in HD; "Godbound themselves never count as lesser foes". — [Just The Rules: Godbound](https://www.kjd-imc.org/blog/just-the-rules-godbound/); [RPG Writeups: Godbound](https://writeups.letsyouandhimfight.com/doresh/godbound/)
- **Tier-gated caps:** Anima allows results above 280 only with Inhumanity, and above 320 only with Zen. — [Ariamaki's Anima writeup](https://writeups.letsyouandhimfight.com/ariamakizeetoo/anima-beyond-fantasy/)
- **Comeback momentum:** in Exalted 3e, a crashed character who crashes their opponent back resets to Initiative 3 plus a Join Battle roll and gets an immediate extra attack. — [Exalted 3E Combat Cheat Sheet](https://exalted3e.obsidianportal.com/wikis/combat-cheat-sheet)
- **Risk-priced bursts:** Double Cross Titus (a one-shot 10-die bonus or rising from 0 HP by spending a bond) — [Faustusnotes](https://faustusnotes.com/2010/07/25/japanese-rpg-double-cross-3-titus-and-lois/). TBZ Kiai (20–30 dice, extra attacks; every point becomes Karma, with Asura at 108) — [Mythcreants](https://mythcreants.com/blog/tenra-bansho-zero-is-fun-insanity/); [TV Tropes](https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/TabletopGame/TenraBanshoZERO)
- **Per-round escalation:** Anima Ki accumulation each turn, halved if acting — [FFG forum archive](https://ffg-forum-archive.entropicdreams.com/topic/1207-ki-accumulation/). Exalted 3e motes return at 5 per combat round — [Onyx Path Mechanics Overview](https://theonyxpath.com/exalted-third-edition-mechanics-overview/)
- **Per-scene clock escalation with threshold bonuses:** Double Cross adds +1d10 per scene, with +1/+2/+3 dice at 60/80/100% and +1 effect level at 100%. — [Faustusnotes](https://faustusnotes.com/2010/07/25/japanese-rpg-double-cross-3-titus-and-lois/); [ダブルクロスのススメ rules summary](https://caunserahomare.hatenablog.com/entry/2023/01/01/205908)
- **Triggered escalation:** Exalted withering hits steal Initiative — [Combat Cheat Sheet](https://exalted3e.obsidianportal.com/wikis/combat-cheat-sheet). Scion Momentum is gained when Conditions cause setbacks — [Scion 2e Cheatsheet](https://scion-origin.fandom.com/wiki/Cheatsheet). TBZ Aiki is given by other players (3 per player per scene, max 1 per action) — [ProfessorProf's TBZ writeup](https://writeups.letsyouandhimfight.com/professorprof/tenra-bansho-zero/)
- **Failure mode:** multiplicative transformations on uncapped stats (DBZ 1999, 2.5× Super Saiyan) "break down" at high power levels. — [RPGnet review](https://www.rpg.net/reviews/archive/classic/rev_2503.phtml); [Dragon Ball Wiki](https://dragonball.fandom.com/wiki/Dragon_Ball_Z:_The_Anime_Adventure_Game)
- **Design stance on visible power:** Exalted Essence built anima so it is "very difficult to keep it down", making visible power the default when you use serious energy. — [Onyx Path Forums: Exalted Essence Questions](https://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/main-category/exalted/1454140-exalted-essence-questions)
- **XP rewards for pushing risk:** Double Cross gives 2 XP for ending at 0–30%, 5 XP for 70–99% and 3 XP for over 100%. — [Panda Gaming Grove](https://pandagaminggrove.blogspot.com/2013/08/double-cross.html)

### Inferences
Design lessons mapped onto the proposed CDZ pillars (these are my synthesis, not sourced claims):
- **Pillar "Cosmo starts low and rises during a fight (damage, falling, allies in danger)".** Combine a *triggered* gain (Exalted withering / Scion Momentum style: +Cosmo when hit, knocked down or seeing an ally endangered) with a *small per-round floor*, so passive characters still escalate (Anima accumulation; Exalted's 5 motes/round). Add an Exalted-style **comeback reset**: when a Saint "falls" (0 HP or crash), they may rise with a reset Cosmo value plus a roll and an immediate attack. That turns falling into the power-up, which is iconic for the genre. Optionally, TBZ-style player-given "sparks" can reward allies' dramatic moments.
- **Pillar "spend Cosmo on techniques, or burn it to jump a tier at the cost of HP".** Scion's Feat of Scale is the template: one action, a scarce cost, and a themed trigger. Double Cross's Titus and TBZ's Karma show that a burst works best when it carries a **lasting** price beyond HP. For example, a "strain" track checked after the battle in a Backtrack-style roll where bonds (Athena, fellow Saints) supply the dice to recover. Reward pushing close to the edge with XP, as Double Cross does.
- **Pillar "Senses (6th/7th/8th) are tiers that define speed, awakened mid-fight".** Model a Sense as a **cap-breaker** (Anima Inhumanity/Zen: without the 7th Sense, results or speeds above X are impossible) plus a **relative step** (Scion Scale: only the tier *difference* matters, a bounded bonus per step). A Bronze who awakens the 7th Sense against a Gold then fights on even terms, as in the source material. Use Godbound's rule so PCs are **never mooks**, while lower-Sense NPCs can be handled with a Fray-style auto-damage die.
- **Dice proposal "Sense = die size, Cosmo = number of dice".** The sources carry three warnings. (1) Die-size steps give a *multiplicative-feeling* gap per tier, and DBZ 1999 shows multiplicative tier jumps break at the top. Consider a Godbound-style result chart (0/1/2/4 per die) or success-counting (e.g., 6+ = success) so die size raises *reliability and crit range* rather than raw damage. (2) Double Cross grows power mostly by **adding dice at thresholds** (+1/+2/+3). That fits "Cosmo = number of dice" and suggests Cosmo thresholds that add dice or technique levels. (3) Cap dice per technique by Sense (Naruto d20 caps chakra per jutsu at caster level; Exalted caps Excellency dice by Attribute + Ability, per my unverified recollection). Burning Cosmo is then the sanctioned way to exceed the cap, which keeps one-shots across tiers rare but possible.
- **The aura as a visible gauge.** Tie aura level to Cosmo spent or burned (Exalted: +1 level per 5 Peripheral motes; Essence: 2 motes = 1 anima; Active effect at 2, Iconic at 10). Give aura levels both perks (a signature effect at the top) and costs (no stealth or feints, enemies read your technique), with a fixed decay per scene.

### Gaps
- No source directly analysed **win-probability math** for underdogs across tiers in any of these systems. The underdog conclusions above are inferred from rule structure, not from published playtest data.
- No Saint Seiya fan RPGs were examined here (out of scope, and the search budget was exhausted).
- Several exact tables are unverified: DX3 bonuses above 100%, Anima accumulation and technique costs, Godbound Effort per level, the Exalted 3e Excellency and stunt specifics, and the TBZ Kiai menu. They should be checked against the rulebooks before they are quoted as rules.
